I am someone who wakes up every morning with real plan to see what happens next. My hobbies include staring at walls while pretending to think deeply, opening the fridge for no reason, and trying to remember whether I already did the task I’m currently doing.

## No idea?

# Where am I from
I come from a place that definitely exists, though describing it would require effort I do not currently possess. It has buildings, roads, and people who walk around looking busy even when they aren’t. The weather changes whenever it feels like it, usually at the exact moment I step outside without preparation. The location has a name, but remembering it seems unnecessary because it contributes nothing to the global understanding of anything. Simply imagine a place that is not here but also not there, and you’ve basically got it.

## Or is it reachable?
I come from a place that cannot be found on maps, not because it is far, but because it refuses to stay still long enough to be drawn. It is a corner of the universe where time folds gently, where silence has weight, and where shadows stretch farther than they should. It is neither home nor origin, merely a point where existence paused long enough for me to notice it. Imagine a horizon with no sun, a place that feels familiar despite never being seen—that is where I began, or perhaps where I simply appeared.
